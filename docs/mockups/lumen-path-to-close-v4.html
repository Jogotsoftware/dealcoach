Linked-out routes from this widget. Every interactive element above is hooked to its own page (opens in new tab so the deal home stays anchored).
  • /deal/{id}/path — full Path to Close page (the widget's "Full view" link)
  • /deal/{id}/confidence — glass-box page: full factor breakdown, org calibration explanation, "what would move this most"
  • /deal/{id}/barriers/{barrier_id} — barrier detail: full history of the risk, all transcript evidence, history of attempted moves, AI-suggested coaching
  • /deal/{id}/barriers — full barrier list (when the AE clicks "+ 2 more open")
  • /deal/{id}/gate — full Sage gate criteria
  • /deal/{id}/gate/{dimension} — dimension drill-in (Power, Budget, etc.)
  • /deal/{id}/msp — mutual success plan

One inline exception. The "Draft demo invite for Marcus to forward" button does not link out — it opens the Ask Lumen drawer with a templated prompt prefilled. The barrier-detail page is one tab over for context; the action is one click away inline. Most barriers will have an inline action like this; navigating to a page just to click another button defeats the point.

Why this pattern is right for Lumen. The deal home is a control panel. AEs glance at it 10x a day. Detail pages are where work happens — opening them in a new tab means the AE can keep their place on the deal home while they investigate, then close the tab and move on. It also makes every detail page link-shareable (paste into Slack, into 1:1 notes, into MSP comments), which becomes valuable for manager coaching workflows.
